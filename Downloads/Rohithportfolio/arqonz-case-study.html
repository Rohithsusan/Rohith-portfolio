Arqonz Global Pvt Ltd

End-to-End Product Design for Web, Mobile & SaaS Platform

Overview

Arqonz is a comprehensive platform serving both B2C and B2B markets, requiring a unified yet flexible design system that accommodates different user needs and technical capabilities. My role involved working across the entire product ecosystem — website, mobile application, and SaaS platform — to create cohesive, intuitive experiences that reduce cognitive load and improve usability for diverse user segments.

Background & Problem

Arqonz needed a design solution that could serve two distinct audiences: non-technical B2C users who required simplicity and guidance, and well-informed B2B users who needed efficiency and advanced functionality. The existing platform struggled with:

  • High interaction cost and cognitive load for non-technical users
  • Complex navigation that overwhelmed B2C users while limiting B2B users
  • Inconsistent design patterns across web, mobile, and SaaS platforms
  • Lack of clear information hierarchy and visual guidance
  • Difficulty in supporting both user types without compromising either experience

Users

👥 B2C Users

Non-technical users who needed clear guidance, simple workflows, and visual cues to complete tasks. They preferred step-by-step processes, minimal decision points, and intuitive interfaces that didn't require prior knowledge of the platform.

🏢 B2B Users

Business professionals with domain expertise who needed quick access to advanced features, efficient workflows, and comprehensive data visualization. They valued speed, functionality, and the ability to customize their experience.

🔑 Key Challenge: Designing a single platform that could elegantly serve both user types without creating separate experiences or overwhelming either segment.

Design Goals

🧠 Reduce Cognitive Load 🧭 Simplify Navigation ✨ Improve Clarity 🪜 Ensure Usability for Low-Tech Users ⚡ Maintain Efficiency for Power Users 🎯 Create Consistency

Research: I attended business meetings with clients to understand their requirements, pain points, and expectations. I conducted user interviews, analyzed existing usage patterns, and identified key friction points in the current experience. This research informed the design strategy and helped prioritize features based on user needs.

Wireframing: I created low-fidelity wireframes to map out information architecture and user flows. The wireframes focused on simplifying navigation paths, reducing steps in critical workflows, and establishing clear visual hierarchy.

Design System: I developed a comprehensive design system that ensured consistency across web, mobile, and SaaS platforms. The system included typography scales, color palettes, spacing guidelines, component libraries, and interaction patterns.

Final UI Directions

Clean, minimal interfaces with clear visual hierarchy

Key Improvements

  • Simplified Navigation: Reduced primary navigation items and adaptive secondary patterns
  • Progressive Disclosure: Shows essential features first, advanced options on demand
  • Visual Hierarchy: Typography, spacing, and color guide user attention
  • Contextual Help: Inline guidance and tooltips without overwhelming interface
  • Consistent Patterns: Reusable components across all platforms
  • Responsive & Accessible: Mobile-first, improved contrast & keyboard navigation

Final UI Screens

The final designs featured clean, minimal interfaces with plenty of white space, clear typography, and subtle visual cues. Key screens included: streamlined dashboard with customizable widgets, simplified onboarding flows, clear data visualization, intuitive forms with inline validation, efficient navigation patterns, and consistent component library.

The redesigned platform successfully addressed the core challenges: reduced cognitive load through simplified information architecture, improved usability for non-technical users while maintaining efficiency for power users, created a cohesive experience across web, mobile, and SaaS platforms, established a scalable design system that supports future growth, and enhanced user satisfaction through clearer navigation and more intuitive interactions. The design system I created continues to guide product development, ensuring consistency and quality as the platform evolves.

Learnings

This project taught me the importance of understanding diverse user needs and finding elegant solutions that serve multiple audiences. Key learnings included: User Research is Critical — direct engagement revealed insights assumptions couldn't; Progressive Disclosure Works — layering allows one interface to serve different users; Design Systems Enable Scale — well-defined systems accelerate development; Simplicity Requires Thought — reducing complexity while maintaining functionality needs careful consideration; Testing Validates Decisions — usability testing provided concrete feedback; Business Context Matters — understanding business goals is essential for real-world solutions.

This experience strengthened my end-to-end product design approach, from initial research through final implementation, and reinforced my belief in the power of thoughtful, user-centered design.
