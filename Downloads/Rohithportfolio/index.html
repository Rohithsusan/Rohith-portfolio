Arqonz Global Pvt Ltd

I worked on Arqonz' website, mobile app, and SaaS platform. This role strengthened my end-to-end product design approach — from brainstorming to ideation, wireframing, design systems, prototyping, and testing. I also attended business meetings to understand client requirements and represented the design team.

A major design goal was reducing interaction cost and cognitive load for two different audiences: non-technical users in B2C and well-informed business users in B2B. I redesigned flows to simplify navigation, improve clarity, and ensure the product supported both user types without overwhelming them.
